Ravioles

This is a classic pasta fullfild by diferents types of food

Ingredients

  • Flour
  • Eggs
  • Oil
  • cheese

Instructions

  1. Ravioli Yield: ~16-32 ravioli, depending on size and whether you do 1 or 2 rows of filling per sheet. It’s enough for 4 ppl as a light first course or main meal.
  2. Drain store-bought ricotta. Otherwise the ravioli will be soggy.
  3. Think ahead. Drain the ricotta overnight and pick a pasta sauce to serve. I tackle both 1-2 days in advance.
  4. Make space. A large, clutter-free work surface is ideal to roll out the pasta dough. Don’t overlook this tip. Pasta sheets can measure up to 3ft. long!
